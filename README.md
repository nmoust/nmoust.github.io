## 6. Research Interests

- Nanotechnology-driven light-induced processes for environmental protection and solar energy conversion.

- Photocatalytic CO2 reduction to solar fuels (artificial photosynthesis).

- Photocatalytic decomposition and degradation of aqueous and gaseous pollutants.

- Synthesis and characterization of nano-structured photocatalysts for environmental applications.

- Design and construction of high throughput photoreactors and of various experimental setups.

- Incorporation of artificial intelligence computational methods and additive manufacturing (3D printing) in (photo)catalysis.

- Development of high-throughput technologies, catalyst screening, robot-assisted material synthesis.
